## Judging

| Stage | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| Stage One | Pass/fail on fit with theme and use of required APIs |
| Stage Two | Four equally weighted criteria (below) |

**Judges may score from text, images, and video alone, and need not watch past 3 minutes.**  
**Judges:** NVIDIA, Nebius, and Tavily staff.

---

## Stage Two Criteria (Equal Weight)

| Criterion | Official Question (Paraphrased) | What to Show |
|-----------|----------------------------------|--------------|
| **Technological Implementation** | How well is it built, and how effectively does it use Token Factory / AI Cloud models and Nemotron? | End-to-end pipeline, real Nebius job logs, clean repo, one-command run |
| **Design** | A complete, coherent product experience, not a technical proof of concept. | One polished flow with loading, empty, and error states |
| **Potential Impact** | A credible, specific case for a real problem and a real audience, addressed by what is demonstrated. | One precise audience and one failure class shown on screen. No invented market sizes. |
| **Quality of the Idea** | A creative, non-obvious use of Nebius and Nemotron, with real understanding of the problem space. | The verification-first loop, the honest benchmark, and accurate related work |
